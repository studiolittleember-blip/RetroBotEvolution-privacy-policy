# RetroBoy Evolution – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 11/11/2027

RetroBoy Evolution ("the app") is a Game Boy, Game Boy Color and Game Boy Advance emulator for
Android, made by Little Ember Studio ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when
you use the app.

## In short

- The app has **no account** and **no server of its own**. We don't receive your games, saves,
  settings or any other personal information.
- The **free version shows ads from Google AdMob**. Google collects some device information to
  show and measure those ads (see "Ads").
- **Premium is bought through Google Play.** We never see your payment details.
- A few optional features connect to the internet or to nearby devices. Each is described below.

## Information that stays on your device

Your game library (the folders and files you choose), battery saves, save states, cheats,
touch-control layouts and settings are stored in the app's own storage on your device. Screenshots,
video and GIF recordings, Game Boy Printer pictures and exported saves are saved where you choose
or in your Pictures, Movies or Download folders. None of this is sent to us.

Uninstalling the app deletes its own storage. Files saved to shared folders (pictures,
recordings, exported saves) stay until you delete them.

## Ads (free version)

The free version shows a banner in the game library, an occasional full-screen ad when you leave
a game, and an optional ad you can watch to try Premium for one hour. The ads are provided by
**Google AdMob**. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ads software may collect
or receive information such as:

- your device's **advertising ID**,
- your **IP address** (from which an approximate location can be worked out),
- device and app information (for example the device model, Android version and app version),
- how you interact with the ads, and diagnostic information.

On Android versions that support it, Google's ads software may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox
features for ads (ad topics and ad measurement).

**Your choices:**
- In the **European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland**, the app asks for your
  consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your
  choice at any time in **Settings › Premium › Ad privacy choices**.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android
  device settings.
- **Premium** removes the ads: the app doesn't request ads for Premium players.

How Google uses this information:
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Purchases (Premium)

Premium is a one-time purchase made through **Google Play**. Google Play handles the payment; we
don't receive your name, card or other payment details. The app asks Google Play whether Premium
was bought on your Google account, so Premium also works after reinstalling or on a new phone.
See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Updates

If you installed the app from Google Play, it asks Google Play on your phone whether a newer
version exists, and Google Play downloads and installs it when you choose to update. This goes
through Google Play only; nothing is sent to us.

## Box art and the game database (optional)

When **Settings › Library › Download box art** is on (it is on by default), the app:

- downloads the public No-Intro game list from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) to recognize
  your games, and
- downloads cover pictures from the libretro thumbnail server (`thumbnails.libretro.com`). Each
  request contains the name of the game whose cover is needed.

Like any internet request, these servers see your IP address. The app sends nothing else about
you. You can turn box art off, or limit it to Wi-Fi, in Settings.

## Camera (Game Boy Camera games only)

The app asks for camera access only when a game made for the Game Boy Camera starts. The camera
picture is passed to the game while you play; it is not stored or sent anywhere. A picture is
only saved if you choose to: by printing it with the emulated Game Boy Printer or by taking a
screenshot.

## Link play (optional)

Link play connects two phones directly over your local Wi-Fi, a phone's hotspot or Bluetooth
(with phones you have already paired). To play, the second player's game file and save can be
sent to the host phone, and the save is sent back. This goes directly between the two phones and
never through the internet or to us. On Wi-Fi, the phones find each other with a short message on
your local network, which shows the name you chose for link play and the game being played.
Bluetooth permission is only
used to connect to phones you have already paired; the app doesn't scan for Bluetooth devices or
use your location.

## Android's phone backup

When **Settings › Save files › Include in phone backup** is on (the default), Android's own backup
(run by Google if you have enabled it on your phone) and transfers to a new phone can include
your settings, game library, saves and save states, and, when there's room, the game files you
imported and their covers. Turn the setting off to keep them only on your device.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information
from children, and the app itself doesn't collect personal information from anyone.
